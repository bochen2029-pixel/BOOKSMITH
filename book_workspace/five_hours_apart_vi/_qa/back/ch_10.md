#### notes ch_10
- "Cứ cho đó là thứ Sáu": reads as a hypothetical/concessive "just suppose it's Friday"; an odd way to open narration unless the hedge is intended.
- "tuần lệch": opaque to a native reader without context; "lệch" = skewed/offset, the referent (time-zone offset? a shifted week?) is not clear.
- "Thế thì không phải là không.": double negative reads two ways ("that's not a no" vs "it isn't not a secret").
- "anh nhìn cô làm việc đó thật giỏi": can parse as "watches her do that very well" or "watches her work (at) that very well"; the referent of "đó" is unclear.
- "– Nghe nói có xe đón lúc bảy giờ mười lăm.": he says "I hear" about a car booked for her; slightly odd but readable as teasing.
