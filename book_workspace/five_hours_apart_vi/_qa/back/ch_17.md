#### notes ch_17
- Capitalized codes are partly Vietnamized (PHÒNG, TRÍ, ĐÈN, ĐẠI DƯƠNG, ANH) and partly English (LONDON, DAL, LHR, BADGE, IRIS); "nhỏ" is lowercase here but "NHỎ" in ch_16.
- "rút lại: 0": unclear whether the cost of taking back is zero or nothing was taken back.
- "khắc: không gì" beside "khắc: không" elsewhere: "nothing" vs "no" reads as a deliberate shift, but it is easy to miss.
- "vì sao: không có dòng": terse; reads "why: no line" without saying whose line or why none.
