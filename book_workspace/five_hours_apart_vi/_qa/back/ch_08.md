# Back-translation (vi-VN → English), blind, half A: ch_08

### ch_08.001
## VIII. Four in the morning

### ch_08.002
Upstairs, in a room on the twenty-second floor with the curtains left open, Iris Wren does not sleep.

### ch_08.003
She has taken off her shoes, and only that. The suitcase stands against the wall where the porter left it, tilted on its wheel. The coat lies on the chair. The bed is still untouched [lit. "still has its creases intact"], and she sits at the foot of the bed, hands resting in her lap, looking out at the city.

### ch_08.004
From here the horse is almost at eye level, a few blocks away, turning. A tram [tàu điện] runs along Pacific Avenue, two cars, lit and empty, then the buildings swallow it. The freeway flows white one way and red the other. Far off, where he had pointed, the sky over the airport is a murky amber that does not change at all.

### ch_08.005
Her phone lies face down on the nightstand. She does not turn it over.

### ch_08.006
The horse turns. She watches it go around once, then once more, and if she is counting, the number does not matter. Once she looks at the coat on the chair, once at the suitcase, once at her own hands, then out at the city again. She does not get up. She does not try to sleep. She knows how this goes [lit. "She knows this matter already"].

### ch_08.007
At 4:31 by her body and 11:31 by the clock on the nightstand, she is still sitting there, with the horse turning and the city spread out to the very edge, and she is not waiting for anything, and nothing is about to come.

#### notes ch_08
- "nơi anh đã chỉ" ("where he had pointed") refers back to a pointing gesture that does not appear in ch_06 or ch_07 as I read them; a reader may wonder when he pointed.
- "Giường vẫn còn nguyên nếp" reads as "the bed is still made / not slept in"; fine, slightly literary.
