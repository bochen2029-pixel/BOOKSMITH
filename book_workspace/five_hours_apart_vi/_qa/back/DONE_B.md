## Conventions the moderator should know
- Repeated Vietnamese phrases have one fixed rendering each, so you can trace the echoes:
  - "Chẳng có gì sắp đến" → "Nothing is about to come" (part_2 title, ch_11.011, ch_14.012, ch_18.011; "nghe nó sắp đến" in ch_12.051 → "hears it about to come").
  - "Cứ cho (đó) là" → "Just suppose" (ch_10.002, part_3 title, ch_15.002–003, ch_17.007, ch_18.009).
  - "đáng đồng tiền bát gạo" → "worth the coin and the rice bowl [= earned its keep]".
  - "tuần lệch" → "the offset week"; "dự kiến lệch 1 h" → "expectation offset 1 h".
- Log and field terms:
  - "chốt" → latch
  - "khắc" → engrave
  - "lặng" → still
  - "đẩy" → push
  - "làn" → lane
  - "dự kiến" → expect / expectation
  - "khớp" → match
  - "rẽ nhánh / nhánh" → branch
  - "nhập" → merge
  - "bỏ" → discard
  - "tua lại" → rewind
  - "hạng nặng" → heavy
  - "nút" → knot (a reader may also hear "button" or "node")
  - "biên" (the on-screen metric) and "rìa" (the edge of the sheet, and the critical edge) → both "edge"
  - "tấm" → sheet
  - "trường" → field
  - "phòng" → room
