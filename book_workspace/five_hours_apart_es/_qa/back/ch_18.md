#### notes ch_18
- "no tiene yo.": unidiomatic; standard Spanish would be "no tiene un yo" (has no self); bare "yo" reads like a grammar slip.
- "si algo era como algo para ella": "para ella" (for the thing) is easily read as "for her" (Iris), which changes the sense.
- "Es lo único en el cuarto que es igual a lo que hay afuera del cuarto": the subject is unstated (the darkness? the window? she?).
- "le vas a mandar un mensaje con lo que haga": "haga" has no subject; earlier units make it the field, but here it could be read as her.
