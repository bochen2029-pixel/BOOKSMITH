#### notes ch_10
- "Se ganó el pan —dice." / "—Sí que se lo ganó." The subject is never named; a reader cannot tell whether it is the week, the norther, or a person who "earned its bread".
- "se sentaron los dos en tu escritorio": "en tu escritorio" can read as sitting on top of the desk; "frente a tu escritorio" or "a tu escritorio" would be unambiguous.
- "el primer pesado": aviation jargon ("heavy"); a general es-419 reader may not know "pesado" names a heavy jet.
- "la semana desfasada": opaque without earlier context; reads as a coined label.
- "P4, donde está tu carro" then "Las puertas se abren al fondo del estacionamiento": slightly unclear whether the elevator stops at P4 or continues below it ("y luego sigue" suggests it continues).
