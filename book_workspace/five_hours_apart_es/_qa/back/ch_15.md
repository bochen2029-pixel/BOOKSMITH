#### notes ch_15
- "pantalla  interior 290 K  exterior 0.003 K": "pantalla" reads first as a display screen; if a shield or shell is meant, "escudo" or "coraza" would be clearer.
- "como P4, océano a vaso de agua": telegraphic to the point of opacity; the proportion it states (which is to which) is unclear.
- "lo que escribe no puede tallarlo": "lo" can be the field or the thing written, so the rule reads two ways.
- "cuarto" (in "ver cuarto", "pagada vía: cuarto", "cuartos corridos"): room, fourth or quarter; the log gives no context to settle it.
