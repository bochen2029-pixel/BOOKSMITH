#### notes ch_17
- "pensar   deshecho: 0": "deshecho" (undone) sits next to "energía gastada" and "cuesta calor", where "desecho" (waste) would also fit; possible typo, and the two readings differ.
- "se funde  vuelve a crecer": "fundirse" can mean melt, fuse/merge, or burn out; the log uses "fusiona" elsewhere for merge, so the different verb invites a different reading.
- "IRIS  despierta  lo lee": "despierta" reads as verb (wakes) or adjective (awake).
- "(un martes que escribió)": no subject; the reader cannot tell who wrote the Tuesday.
- "ÉL  dormido  norte de Dallas": in a log line, "norte de Dallas" reads as "north of Dallas" (outside the city) rather than North Dallas.
