#### notes ch_16
- "(él dijo que a eso le importaría)" and "“A eso le importaría.”": reads two ways (that thing would care / it would matter to that); the line is load-bearing, so the ambiguity matters.
- "córrelo.  escríbela.": the gendered clitics fix a masculine "it/him" and a feminine "it/her"; the reader cannot tell if a neutral "it" was meant.
- "10-27 22:41:12  ÉL  “Son las once.”": "Son las once" can only be a clock time, and it is logged at 22:41.
- "10-30 19:05:12  ÉL  “No sé.”": in ch_12 "No sé" is said before the machines breathe and the 19:04:43 fork, but here it is logged after it.
- "nivel seis, la suya abierta": "la suya" has no stated feminine antecedent (its forecast? hers?).
