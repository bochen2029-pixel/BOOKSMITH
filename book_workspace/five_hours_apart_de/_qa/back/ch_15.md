#### notes ch_15
- "Raum" in the log rows ("Quellen 1 (siehe Raum)", "bezahlt über: Raum", "Räume gelaufen 7.2e8") (ch_15.006): reads as both "room" and "(outer) space"; the reader cannot tell which is meant.
- "SKY/Schein" (ch_15.006): "Schein" is opaque here (glow? shine? semblance?); a more specific noun would help.
- "Schirm  innen 290 K  außen 0.003 K" (ch_15.004): "Schirm" can be screen, shield, umbrella or lampshade.
- "was es schreibt, kann es nicht prägen" (ch_15.005): subject and object "es" can swap; the rule can be read in two opposite directions.
- "Stunden übrig: einige" (ch_15.004): "hours left: some" reads oddly for a star; unclear if intentional.
