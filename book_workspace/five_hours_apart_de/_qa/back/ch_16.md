#### notes ch_16
- Title „Raum“ (ch_16.001) and the row labels "Raum ..." (ch_16.002, .004, .006): the German cannot distinguish "room" from "space", and the unit leans on the word.
- „Regel 3   ihn laufen lassen.  sie schreiben.“ (ch_16.003): "sie schreiben" reads both as "they write" and as the instruction "write her"; only "IRIS — geschrieben (Regel 3)" hints at the second.
- Codes are half translated: ER, GELÄNDER, VATER, WETTER, KLEIN are German while SKY, ORGAN, WORLD, CLOCK, BODY stay English; "ER" as a speaker tag reads oddly to a German reader.
- „wie eine Liste von Noten über ein Lied stimmt“ (ch_16.006): "über etwas stimmen" is not idiomatic (expected "auf ein Lied zutrifft"), though a pun on "stimmen" (to tune) may be intended.
- „(der kleine hört zu)“ (ch_16.002): unclear who "der kleine" is (receiver? small room?).
