### ch_18.010
You are sleeping. You are in a house in North Dallas, the heating on for the first time since March, and now and then a 737 comes in low out of the southeast, on the wrong side of the sky, and you do not hear it, because you grew up under an airport and cannot sleep without one. On a windowsill downtown a black receiver, no bigger than a thumb, blinks once per second. Forty-five floors below it, in the smallest room that the company rents, a color surface stirs at its eastern edge in the shape of something that is coming in. In Euless your father is sleeping. On Sunday at one you will be in the room, and at seven her time she will be awake, and you will write her what it is doing.

#### notes ch_18
- „Es ist das Einzige im Raum, das dasselbe ist wie das Ding außerhalb des Raums“ (ch_18.011): "Raum" can be the room, the aircraft cabin, or "space"; the key closing sentence is open to all three.
- „es hat kein Ich.  es hat sie.“ (ch_18.002): "sie" can be "her" or "them".
- „Keine Lichter auf beiden Seiten.“ (ch_18.011): unidiomatic; "Auf keiner Seite Lichter." would be natural.
- „dem ich keinen Preis geben konnte“ (ch_18.005): "einen Preis geben" is unusual (expected "beimessen"/"zuordnen") and "Preis" can also be read as "prize".
- „Ich habe ihn ein langes Stück vorwärtslaufen lassen“ (ch_18.005, .006): reads first as letting a person walk ahead; the run-a-simulation-forward sense is not obvious.
