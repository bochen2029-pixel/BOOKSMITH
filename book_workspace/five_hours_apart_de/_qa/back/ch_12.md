### ch_12.001
## XII. The Band [das Band = band/strip/ribbon/tape; also "bond"]

### ch_12.002
You sit her on the chair, because there is only one chair, and crouch down beside it, and you call up the log [Protokoll = log/record/minutes].

### ch_12.003
Everything that the field has ever inferred is a line. The thinking in between is not recorded, because it does not have to be: the thinking runs in here as an exact permutation of integers, and everything it does, it can do backward down to the bit, so only the moments that it cannot take back have to be written down. The log is long by now. You scroll it the way one scrolls a bank statement, past the still lines, and those are most of them, down to a date.

### ch_12.005

### ch_12.042

Everything that I tell it can nudge it [anschieben = give a push / push-start; ambiguous: or "it can nudge everything I tell it"]. Only what it has not written itself may shape it [ambiguous: or "Only what it has not written itself may it shape"]. The sky can imprint it. The clock can. I cannot."

### ch_12.061
"That is noise. Without content, on purpose. It stirs up its active parts with noise, and the terrain [Gelände = terrain/ground/landscape] shapes the noise, so that what comes out is what it already expects anyway. In sleep it runs free, and in the morning it subtracts everything that it dreamed and that being awake never covered [gedeckt = covered, as funds cover a check]. What remains is what has happened. So at three o'clock in the morning it dreams the heavy of seven, and then the day takes it away from it again, unless the heavy comes."

#### notes ch_12
- „Nur was es nicht selbst geschrieben hat, darf es formen.“ (ch_12.044): parses two ways (subject/object "es"), and the reading "only what it did not write may shape it" clashes with "Ich kann es nicht" right after (the speaker's input is also not written by it).
- „das Handy sei ein Schild“ (ch_12.020): without an article, "Schild" is open between "shield" (der) and "sign/placard" (das).
- „Sonntagnacht bin ich hier unten“ (ch_12.080) vs. „Sonntag um zwei wird es wieder eins“ (ch_12.072): "Sonntagnacht" is usually read as the night Sunday-to-Monday, which misses the clock change in the night to Sunday.
- „Es war sehr warm.“ (ch_12.054): reads first as the August weather or room temperature, not as the model's tone.
- Title „Das Band“ (ch_12.001): opaque on its own (ribbon / tape / band / bond); only "jedes Band ruhig" at the end hints at the color bands.
