## 3. Recommendations on register (which words to keep plain inside the fiction)

1. **Keep the narrator's lay words lay.** The story never says vortex, antivortex, screening length, Langevin, Landauer, renormalization, MDL, copy-on-write or ADS-B; it says pinwheel, partner, reach, settle, rent, page table, receiver. Translate at the same altitude: 風車 / 伴 / 搆得到 / 落定 / 租 / 分頁表(頁表) / 接收器. The technical columns above are for the translator's certainty, not for the page.
2. **The three words that must stay technical because the character says them as an engineer:** hash (雜湊/哈希), bits/bytes (位元·位元組 / 比特·字节), page table (分頁表 / 页表). These are also three of the hardest TW/CN splits, so the zh-Hans derivation cannot be a script conversion; it needs a term pass.
3. **Rows:** translate verbs and values (成核 / 釘 / 落定 / 靜 / 醒 / 攪 / 刻 / 分叉 / 倒轉 / 丟棄 / 預期 / 應驗 / 開 / 已認證 / 精確), keep identifiers Latin (`E/1931`, `NE/6`, `SKY`, `ORGAN`, `ckpt 2c71…`, `seed 9e40…`, `#8812`, `n_f(S)`, `t 1.0e13 y`). Column alignment matters more than literalness; the rows are read as a machine's voice.
4. **Row vs 行/列:** use 筆 for "row" in prose ("每一個結論都是一筆") and 紀錄 N 筆 in rows; this is the one place where a literal choice would be wrong for half the readership.
5. **Two "edges":** the KT edge (XI, "just inside the edge") → 邊緣; the field's boundary (XII, "wired to the edge") → 邊界.
6. **One word for "heavy"** (重型機) across the father, the narrator and the rows; XIII turns on their saying it the same way.
7. **"Nothing is due"**: keep the ledger sense (到期); it binds "rent", "expect … open/met" and the title of Part Two.
8. **Settle → 落定** rather than 安定/沉降: in the spec a settle *is* a conclusion (§2.3), and 落定 already means "decided".

## 4. What could not be resolved (moderator action)

- No page from NAER (terms.naer.edu.tw) could be fetched, so the TW official forms for 克耳文, 釘扎, 遮蔽長度, 詢答機, 分頁表 and 成核 rest on standard usage and located-but-unfetched TW pages (confidence M-H), not on a quoted entry.
- TW transliterations of Kosterlitz (茲 vs 茨) and Berezinskii are unconfirmed; the CN forms are confirmed by thepaper.cn. One fetch of cna.com.tw/news/firstnews/201610040413.aspx from an unblocked machine settles it.
- Whether to keep the lane names (SKY, ORGAN, WORLD, BADGE, CLOCK, BODY) in Latin inside the Chinese rows is a design decision, not a term question; this report recommends Latin identifiers with Chinese verbs/values.
- "the exact plane" is a spec title (§6) and never surfaces in the story's prose; only the row token `exact` does. No Chinese form needs to be fixed for it beyond 精確.
- Josephson 接面/结 and helicity modulus (螺旋度模量) were not needed by the story and are listed only for translator's notes.
