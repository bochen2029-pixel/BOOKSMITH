## 2. Overrides of the key

None. Every H and M row fired in its locked form. Two places where the key was silent and a choice had to be made that
touches the charter's own models:

- **ch_07.002** "a quarter past eleven" → 星期二晚上十一點一刻 (the charter §6 model is the bare 十一點一刻). 晚上 is the
  period word the Chinese 12-hour clock needs, not a gloss, and without it 星期二十一點一刻 is read as 二十一點 (21:00).
  GT_PRECEDENT's ★ model for this sentence also carries 晚上.
- **ch_07.009** tag "she asks" → 她問. The charter §5 says the tag is 說; the source's verb is "asks", and 問 (not 問道)
  is its plain rendering. If the moderator wants 說 everywhere, the line becomes 「你會離開嗎？」她說。「達拉斯。」

## 3. Choices the moderator should look at

- **ch_07.002** "the stoplights go through their changes for nobody" → 紅綠燈一輪一輪地變換，不為任何人. 一輪一輪 carries
  "go through their changes" (the cycle); 不為任何人 lands last as "for nobody" does and sits beside the locked
  不為任何人亮著 (glowing for no one) without copying it.
- **ch_07.002** "a patio" → 露天座位區 (GT_TERMS form). ch_02 has "On a patio someone laughs"; the two units should
  carry one noun. 露台 was rejected (a terrace, not street-level seating one can hose into the street).
- **ch_07.003** "forgotten it's there" → 忘了它的存在. 忘了它在那裡 misreads as "forgot where it is".
- **ch_07.003** "the way she came through the doors" → 穿過那道門時: the frosted arrivals doors of ch. IV (heading 「四、門」).
  The ch_04 worker's noun for those doors should be checked against 那道門.
- **ch_07.008** "A train" → 一列電車 (DART light rail at street level; no registry row; GT_NAMES: keep 列車/電車 neutral).
  ch_08's draft already has 一列電車; ch_06 ("a train slides along Pacific Avenue, lit and empty") should match.
  "slides away" → 往西區滑去. The motif form 亮著，空著 is not used here because the English varies it ("lit the way a
  kitchen is lit at three in the morning, with no one on it at all") → 亮得像凌晨三點的廚房，車上一個人也沒有.
- **ch_07.010** 「我想這件事的方式，跟我想學鋼琴一樣。」 想 doubles as "think about" and "want to", so 想學鋼琴 already
  carries the fondness the next line names; nothing was added to get it.
- **ch_07.011** "Which is?" → 「也就是？」. GT_PRECEDENT's model 「怎麼個想法？」 is the 怎麼個…法 construction, which reads
  mainland; 也就是？ is the Taiwanese prompt for "which is to say?".
- **ch_07.014** "steel derrick" → 鋼架 (locked M). The English word quietly points at the oil company of ch. VI; 鋼架
  does not. 鋼製井架 would, but is clumsy in the line; kept the lock.
- **ch_07.014** "From the forty-eighth floor it was a toy" → 從四十八樓看，它是個玩具: the English past tense is carried
  by the viewpoint 從…看, not by 了 or 曾經.
- **ch_07.014** "forty feet of red neon" → 四十呎的紅色霓虹 (extent, not height; no 高 added).
- **ch_07.019** "If I were flying in." → 「如果是我飛進來的話。」 的話 is the conditional ending, not a sentence-final
  particle; 「如果是我飛進來。」 is the alternative if the moderator wants it barer.
- **ch_07.024** "turns again empty" → 又轉一次，空著. 空著 ends the sentence on "empty" as the English does, and rhymes
  with the book's 亮著，空著 exactly as far as the English "empty" rhymes with "lit and empty"; 空的 (v1) was flatter.
- **ch_07.024** "doors open and close on people you didn't know" → 門為你不認識的人開了又關: 為 follows the charter's own
  model for "the doors open on her" (為她打開, §4); 開了又關 is the charter's own 了-aspect example (§3.2).
