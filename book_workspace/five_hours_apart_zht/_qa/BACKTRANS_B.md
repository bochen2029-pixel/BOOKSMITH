001 ## Ten, North Wind

002 Suppose it is Friday, the thirtieth, the last working day of the staggered week, and the first north wind of the year blows in at four forty.

003 From the forty-first floor, you see it before you feel it. The sky to the northwest turns the color of a nickel. On the screen, the temperature outside walks through 79, 71, 64, 57 Fahrenheit in forty minutes, and the cooling windows tick. Past Irving, you don't need to look to know what the airport is doing. It is turning around. A calm voice is telling every plane over the DFW metro area, one plane at a time, that it will now land from the south. The line of lights you have watched all week will form up again on the other side, over Euless, over the house you grew up in, and your father will hear the first heavy pass overhead at a quarter past five and know what that means sooner than the weather does.

002 You walk her across the bottom level of the garage, past your own car, which unlocks as you pass because it thinks you've come to drive it, you haven't, and it locks again. 021 "He has a copy." She looks at you. "The whole thing. Every conclusion it has made since June. On a hard drive in his desk, in Euless, under the flight path. There's one at my place too. 022 "Then it's a record."

023 "Stopped, it's a record. Running, it's alive. The record is the way it outlives me."

026 "You remembered."

027 "It's the most Texas thing I know."

028 At the north end of International Avenue, the car takes the departures ramp, the upper roadway, the one you yourself have never once used, and finds a spot at the curb at Terminal D, between a shuttle and a family unloading an absurd amount of luggage. 029 "I'll have someone look at it back in London," she says. "It's been very loyal."

030 Then you are standing in the cold air at the curb, the terminal lit behind her, the doors behind her sliding open and shut every few seconds for people with somewhere to go.

031 "Message me what it does," she says. "Sunday. That hour."

032 "That'll be two in the morning. Then one."

033 "Either way it's seven for me. I'll be awake."

034 "Seven on a Sunday morning, you'll be awake."

035 "I'll be awake."

036 She holds out her hand, and you take it, the same handshake, exactly the right length.

037 "Sunday," she says.

038 "Sunday."

039 "Thank you," she says. "The room thing." [For the room.]

040 She says your name once more, with that extra syllable, and goes. The doors take her, close, open for someone else, and close again.

## Reader's notes

Everything below is from the Chinese alone. "Guess" means I chose one reading of an ambiguous Chinese phrase; "unclear" means the line did not resolve for me as a reader of this half; "P1 ref" means the line leans on something that must be in Part One, which I have not read.

### A. Places I had to guess

- ch_10 002, ch_14 012, ch_18 009 — 錯開的那一週 rendered "the staggered week". The Chinese says only "the week that is offset / out of step"; ch_18 009 ("the week London is five hours ahead instead of six") is what tells a reader what is offset. Alternatives: "the offset week", "the out-of-step week".
- ch_10 003 — 比天氣更早知道那是什麼意思: read as "know what that means sooner than the weather does"; could also be "know what it means before [he knows] the weather".
- ch_10 004, ch_12 077–079 — 那些六 / 十四個六 "those sixes / fourteen sixes": what a "six" is cannot be recovered from this half (reads as a wrong digit found fourteen times in some files). 079 讓它自己去找它的 "Let it find its own" — its own sixes, presumably; left bare as in the Chinese.
- ch_10 006, 007; ch_12 005; ch_18 011 — 賺回飯錢 rendered literally "earn back its meal money" every time (coat, knot, coat). The idiom means "earn its keep / pay for itself".
- ch_10 008 — 說好的德州呢 "What about the Texas I was promised": 說好的 = "the one that was agreed on"; a mild joke, and it lands.
- ch_11 007 — 原子的事不對 "The atoms part was wrong": elliptical in Chinese ("the atom matter, not right").
- ch_11 012 and throughout — 一片 / 那一片 rendered "sheet". 片 is a flat-thing classifier; the screen object is "a door-sized 片 of color" (ch_11 012), and the same word recurs for the field itself (ch_11 030, ch_12 005, 040, 060, 090, 122, ch_14 005, ch_18 010), for "a 片 of what I think / of what happened" (ch_12 046), for "a 片 of labeled dots" on the map (ch_12 094) and for "a 片 of black" out the aircraft window (ch_18 011). I kept one word so the moderator can see every occurrence; in 094 and ch_18 011 the Chinese is probably only the classifier ("a scatter of dots", "a blackness"), not the field. Alternatives: expanse, patch, plane.
- ch_11 022 — 科斯特利茲／索利斯／別列津斯基 transliterated as Kosterlitz / Thouless / Berezinskii from sound and context (helium films, superconductor arrays). A Taiwanese reader without physics would not recover these names; 索利斯 in particular could be read as "Solis".
- ch_11 024, ch_12 038, 047, 090, ch_14 006 — 前沿 rendered "frontier"; "front" or "leading edge" are equally possible.
- ch_11 041 and after — 黛娜 rendered "Dana"; "Dinah" is the other common value of this transliteration.
- ch_12 001, 002, 003; ch_13 022, 023 — 紀錄帶 "record tape" (chapter title, and ch_15 005, ch_18 008) vs 紀錄 "record" (prose). The Chinese pairs 紀錄帶 with 倒帶 "rewind", which is literally "turn the tape back"; "log" / "ledger" would be the computing reading.
- ch_12 004 — 已認證 "certified": could be "verified" or "authenticated".
- ch_12 012, 037 etc. — 通道 rendered "channel"; the instruction's own word is "lane". The Chinese word is the ordinary word for a passage/channel.
- ch_12 012, 103, ch_14 010 — 應驗 "fulfilled": could be "came true" / "met".
- ch_12 024 — 它比你先到 "It got there before you": 到 = arrive; the alternative "It was there before you" is in brackets. The reply in 025 ("It's always there") fits either.
- ch_12 028–030 — 不為什麼 rendered "Not for anything". Idiomatically it is the stock answer "no reason / for nothing"; as a reply to 027 ("What is it for?") a reader hears "For nothing."
