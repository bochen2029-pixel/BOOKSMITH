### ch_06.001  [h2]

## VI. Forty-Eight

### ch_06.002  [para]

The elevator is mirrored and very fast. Your ears do the thing again, in reverse. Less than three hours ago you came down a tower four blocks from here, alone.

### ch_06.003  [para]

The restaurant is nearly empty at this hour, which makes it feel as though it's been kept open for you: low light, dark wood, white linen, and glass from floor to ceiling. The host walks you to a table against the window, and the city lays itself out below you to its edges. There are the freeways you drove tonight, braided and moving, and the black band of the Trinity. Far off to the west is the faint amber of the airport, where an hour ago you stood with your arm in the air.
