## XIII. Departures

### ch_13.002  [para]

The car is waiting on Commerce with its hazards on, and you tip the driver to go home. The cold has gotten serious while you were underground. You turn on the heat for the first time since March, and the smell of it, dust burning off, fills the car and makes it a different car.

### ch_13.003  [para]

Everything is where it was on Tuesday, and the wind is from the north now, and the city has changed sides.
