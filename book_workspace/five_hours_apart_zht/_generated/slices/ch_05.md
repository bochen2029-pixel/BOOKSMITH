### ch_05.002  [para]

The car unlocks as you approach, and she watches the mirrors unfold with the polite interest of someone who's been awake for twenty hours. You fit the suitcase in the back, broken wheel and all. You take the car down the garage ramps yourself, and out onto International Parkway heading north, and at the first long straight you give it the wheel, and she watches your hands leave it.

### ch_05.024  [para]

After that she goes quiet, the long-flight kind of quiet. It isn't unfriendly. She's simply out of words in the language of small talk, and you let the car fill the silence with the sound of its tires. Las Colinas goes by again, the same lit towers you passed two hours ago going the other way, still glowing for no one.

### ch_05.025  [para]

### ch_05.027  [para]

The car brings you down off the freeway into downtown, where the streets are emptier now, and stops itself at the hotel's curb. A bellman takes the suitcase with its broken wheel, and you feel, absurdly, a pang at handing it over, as if you'd been promoted from stranger to porter and are now being let go.
