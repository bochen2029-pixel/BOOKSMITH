## 1 · The intent

The question was never how to make the box useful, and never how to make it super-intelligent. It was **how to create an alive mind inside a box.**

- **Intelligence at verifiable work is already solved enough.** Frontier language models are super-intelligent at essays, code and proofs, and by the calculator principle they keep that work. A calculator beats a mind at arithmetic, and nobody concludes that minds are pointless. This mind does not compete for the language model's job.
- **The field took two roads, and then only one.** In the 1980s the fork was symbols against networks; since then it has been copying the brain against modelling the words. The transformer won, and the attention of the whole field went with it. An intelligence that arose elsewhere in the universe, not built on biology, would not be a language model. It would be a different structure that is still a mind: it observes its world, maintains itself in it, and thrives there.
- **It must be built from what exists.** The primitives are CPUs and GPUs, programmed in C, C++ and CUDA. Nations spend a decade trying to match one lithography machine, and no lab will invent a new substrate for a mind. So the design works backwards, from the silicon to the mind the silicon is already shaped to hold.
- **"Alive" means its own existence.** It means a persistent, resident existence, native to the graphics card: a pattern that keeps itself going, that is there between events, and that grows from what happens to it.

**The canon said it first.**
- *The Grace in the Gradient* calls living things **"eddies in the descent"**: order that the flow produces rather than fights.
- *The Whole Machine* names **"exactly two deaths"**:
  - the groove that carves one path until it can go nowhere else;
  - the ring that tears itself apart faster than it can settle.

  A mind is what holds the band between them. It rules that **"silence is legal."** And it asks the question every architecture of pure thinking dodges: what the mind turns for **"in the hours when nobody is asking it anything."**

**KELVIN is those sentences made physics.** Its two deaths are two phases of matter. Its band is a phase boundary. Its silence is a quiet tile that costs nothing.

## 3 · The answer, in one paragraph

KELVIN is a two-dimensional XY medium held just inside its Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless phase.
- **Its concepts are topological charges.** A distinction the present can decide is a pinned pair. A distinction held across time is a loop: a ring with one π-bond whose circulation stores a bit and flips on each input, like the toggle flip-flop of superconducting logic.
- **Thinking is an integer permutation** and runs backward to the bit. Concluding erases, and only conclusions are recorded.
- **Abstraction is renormalization.**
- **Learning is waking minus dreaming,** and only consequences the mind did not author may carve its memory.
- **Attention is a screened field:** a thought reaches as far as its consequences, and quiet costs nothing.
- **Practice is crystallization:** what it does often and reliably hardens from field into table, and melts back when the world moves.

The derivation is in Part IV of the paper. Crystallization is new in this note (§9).

## 4 · What alive means here, and how it is tested

Between calls, a language model is a stored function. It shows none of the signs of life, and that is no criticism: it is the calculator principle again. **This mind is judged first on the signs of life, and each one is made measurable.** Capability comes second (§7).

**The aliveness battery (E-ALIVE), the primary kill list:**

| sign of life | the medium's analog | the test | passes if | dies if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **organization** | concepts and loops protected by topology; they cannot drift | **E-IDENTITY** | scramble the terrain and it stops being itself (its predictions and self-observables leave their bands); restore the terrain and both return | scrambling changes nothing: the memory was never in the terrain |
| **homeostasis** | the living band, held region by region | **E-BAND** | a region pushed frozen or turbulent returns inside the band by itself, within a bounded time, with the homeostat's constants untouched | it needs hand-tuning, or never returns |
| **metabolism** | stirring in active regions, logged; cryptobiosis when quiet | **E-EXIST** | over days, quiet costs the card's idle power, and activity costs in proportion to the frontier | existing requires the whole card to run |
| **growth** | concepts born from its own world, by phase slip | **E1, E-SLIP** | a loop is born where futures diverge (the even process), and nothing where they do not (the golden mean) | no births, or births everywhere |
| **response** | a push wakes exactly the tiles it reaches | **E-WAKE, E-ADDRESSED** | the frontier after a push stays inside the reach bound, and what the mind reports about itself matches its field | the whole field wakes, or it can only reconstruct |
| **adaptation** | terrain carved only by consequences | **E-MELT** | a mid-stream change in a source's statistics melts and re-forms the affected region, and the rest stays settled | it stays frozen, or re-forms the wrong region |
| **self-production** | its parts turn over: concepts are born by slip and evicted by rent; skills harden into tables and melt back (§9) | **E-TURNOVER** | over weeks in a changing world it keeps hardening and keeps melting, its melt rate holds its band with the homeostat's constants untouched, and E-IDENTITY's observables hold while the parts are replaced | nothing melts (the groove), skills melt before they repay (the ring), or it stops being itself as its parts are replaced |
| **reproduction** | forks and spores | **E-KIN** | two forks raised for weeks in different worlds diverge in terrain, yet stay closer to each other than to a stranger raised in either world | they never diverge (no individuality), or become strangers (no heredity) |
| **a life of its own** | what the ring turns for when nobody asks | **E-NIGHT** | left with only a clock, its organization persists, and its night work changes the next day's predictions, or what they cost, against a twin whose night was skipped | the skipped twin does as well: nothing was going on in there |

**Two of these run now.** E-BAND and E-EXIST need no concepts. The substrate as built can already be tested for whether it holds itself alive and what it costs to exist. E-IDENTITY and E-NIGHT need a mind raised for a week in the nursery, and E-TURNOVER needs weeks of its life. E-KIN needs two minds.

## 5 · What the medium gives an alive mind

| the need | what the medium gives it |
|---|---|
| **to persist** | presence between events; a quiet region holds its state and computes nothing; a push wakes exactly what it touches |
| **to survive** | a database's ACID properties, applied to a mind (below) |
| **to be cheap to exist** | a thought costs what it reaches, never what the mind weighs |
| **to get cheaper with practice** | what it does often hardens into tables, and a table costs a lookup (§9) |
| **to grow** | video memory is a cache, not a limit; many cards are one mind |
| **to be native** | integer stencils, warp votes and mipmaps now; the tensor cores next (§6) |

**Survival is ACID.** This mind has a database's four guarantees:
- **Atomicity:** a settle commits whole, as a checkpoint and a seed, or not at all.
- **Consistency:** charge is conserved, so no update creates a concept without its contrast or deletes one without an event.
- **Isolation:** forks are copy-on-write, and imagination cannot touch fact.
- **Durability:** the tape. The mind re-derives from its seed and its rows on any card, and outlives every machine it ever ran on.

**The scaling law.** In a dense transformer with $N$ parameters, capacity and the cost of thinking are one number:

$$
\text{cost per token} \;\approx\; 2N \ \text{operations}, \qquad \text{bytes moved per token} \;\approx\; N \times \text{bytes per weight}.
$$

Every token pays for every weight. In the medium, capacity is the number of cells, $L^2$, and the cost of a thought is bounded by its reach:

$$
\text{cell updates per thought} \;\le\; \frac{\lVert s\rVert_1}{\alpha\,\varepsilon},
$$

which is independent of $L$ (the local-push bound). **Capacity and the cost of a thought are separate variables.** The mind grows, and no single thought gets more expensive. Existing costs nothing in compute: a quiet tile executes no instruction.

**Practice is a third variable.** A transformer or a state-space model pays as much for its millionth parity bit as for its first. Here a familiar thought hardens into a table and costs a lookup (§9).

**Video memory is a cache.** A quiet tile is cryptobiotic, and the page table that already implements forks can page it to host memory or NVMe; a wake pages it back. At five bytes a cell, a terabyte of NVMe holds two hundred billion cells. **The mind's size is bounded by storage, not by the card.**

**Many cards, one mind.** Tiles distribute across cards, and only active boundary tiles exchange halos, over NVLink. Stencils are the canonical weakly scaling workload of scientific computing, run today on entire supercomputers. The mind scales out the way a weather model does.

## 6 · Three moves toward the native form

### Move 1 · The whole chip, without losing a bit

The tensor cores hold roughly an order of magnitude more raw arithmetic than the integer units, and v1 leaves them dark. Write each cell as its unit vector $(c_i, s_i) = (\cos\vartheta_i, \sin\vartheta_i)$. Then the force on cell $i$ is

$$
F_i \;=\; \sum_j J_{ij}\sin(\vartheta_j-\vartheta_i) \;=\; c_i\,S_i - s_i\,C_i,
\qquad
C_i = \sum_j J_{ij}\,c_j,\quad S_i = \sum_j J_{ij}\,s_j .
$$

The fields $C$ and $S$ are linear in the unit-vector field. **Everything the medium aggregates is matrix multiplication, and only the final combination is elementwise.**

**What moves to the tensor cores:**
- **Dense coupling at coarse levels.** Give a coarse level all-to-all learned links. At level 6 of a 4096² base (4,096 coarse cells), the coupling matrix is 16.8 MB in INT8. **This buys the relational reach that two-dimensional locality lacks.**
- **Imagination, batched.** $B$ forks share one coupling matrix, so their fields are one GEMM, $[C\,|\,S] = K\,[\,U_1 \cdots U_B\,]$. At $B = 64$ on level 6 that is about $2\times10^{9}$ multiply-adds per step: tens of microseconds on the card under the desk.
- **Learning dense coupling.** The Boltzmann rule on those links is a difference of outer products, $\Delta K = \eta\,(U_{\text{wake}}U_{\text{wake}}^{\top} - U_{\text{sleep}}U_{\text{sleep}}^{\top})/n$.
- **Operators, readouts and rent audits:** the low-rank operator library, linear readouts, and the batched ablation forks of the rent audit.

**What stays on the integer units:** nearest-neighbour bonds with learned per-edge couplings. That work is elementwise, and bandwidth-bound regardless.

**Exact, so replay survives.** Store $c$, $s$ and $J$ as INT8 and accumulate in INT32.
- Every product is at most $127^2 = 16{,}129$, so a sum of up to 133,000 terms cannot overflow.
- Integer addition is exact in any order.
- A shear on integers is invertible for any integer force function, so exploration stays a permutation.

**Kill (E-TC):** the tensor-core path must match its own integer-ALU reference bit for bit across tile orders and launch slicing, and must run the dense parts faster.

### Move 2 · A substrate that grows itself

In v1, a kernel runs a χ² test and stamps concepts, so the intelligence of growth sits in a classical statistic beside the medium. The physics grows concepts by itself.
- **In a film of the kind the medium models,** a current exerts force on bound vortex pairs and unbinds the larger ones. That is the origin of its nonlinear current–voltage law (Halperin and Nelson, 1979).
- **In a Josephson array,** a junction whose current exceeds its critical current slips its phase by a full turn, and a vortex crosses it.

**In the integer medium, a phase slip is already visible.** It is the moment a shared edge's wrapped difference crosses half a turn, ±128. The two plaquettes flanking that edge change charge by +1 and −1: a pair is born straddling the bond. Only the cause is new.

**Nucleation by overstress, certification by rent:**
- A bond's current is $I_e = J_e \sin\Delta_e$, one table lookup.
- A bond is **overstressed** when $|I_e|$ exceeds its critical value, set by its coupling and the local pinning, for $k$ consecutive settles: where the world keeps pushing and the terrain will not give.
- A slip on an overstressed bond nucleates a **candidate.** Thermal pairs flicker; candidates persist, because the stress that made them persists.
- The rent audit keeps a candidate only if it pays for itself in bits. The χ² table audits the births. **It no longer authors them.**

**Birth and memory are one mechanism.** The superconducting toggle flip-flop flips by a phase slip in one of two junctions, and the circulating current chooses which. **A concept is born by a slip, and a memory ticks by slips.** That is growth in the biological sense: the organism makes its own structure.

**Kill (E-SLIP):** on the gauntlet, with χ² stamping switched off, slip-born concepts must grow the parity loop on the even process and nothing that pays rent on the golden mean.

### Move 3 · The medium's own body

The medium is the lattice model of a circuit that exists.
- **1981:** the transition was observed in arrays of superconducting islands joined by weak links (Resnick et al.).
- **1991:** superconducting logic began computing with the same flux quanta (Likharev and Semenov).

A graphics card emulates the medium with instructions: tens of nanojoules per cell update in today's kernel, and a few at the K1 target. An array would run it as physics, at about $I_c\Phi_0 \approx 10^{-19}$ J per switch, and roughly a thousandfold more once 4-kelvin refrigeration is paid for. **That is still orders of magnitude below the emulator.**

The trade: physical noise is real noise, so a device gives up bit-exact re-derivation of its settles, and its durability rests on recorded checkpoints instead. The division of labour follows:
- **the device settles,** with thermal noise supplied free;
- **the emulator explores,** reversibly and in integers;
- **the tape records** what the device concluded.

This is the Minecraft CPU in reverse: a mind whose native body exists, run on an emulator until that body is built.

## 7 · Capability, second

Capability is not the goal, but a living thing must cope with its world. **The rival here is not a turn-based language model.** It is a recurrent or state-space model running continuously on a stream, of the Mamba or RWKV kind: resident too, at a constant cost per step.

| | an online state-space model | KELVIN |
|---|---|---|
| activity | the whole state updates every step | only the frontier updates; quiet regions compute nothing |
| concepts | continuous; they drift under training | integer charges and loops; they change only by events |
| memory under distraction | decays unless its gates learn to shut perfectly | a loop behind an energy barrier; noise below the barrier cannot flip it |
| learning | backpropagation through time, or its approximations | local wake−sleep and a verdict trace; no backward pass |
| replay | floating point; not bit-exact across hardware | integer; bit-exact on any card |
| practice | its millionth familiar step costs what its first did | familiar skills harden into tables, and the card can sleep while they run |

**Kill (E-SSM).** KELVIN runs against an online state-space model with matched memory and matched joules, with energy measured by integrating board power. Three measures:
1. **capability per joule,** in bits of prediction per joule (§9), on the gauntlet and a streaming world;
2. **samples to competence;**
3. **retention under distraction:** a bit written at step 0, read after $T$ steps of irrelevant input, with $T$ from $10^2$ to $10^6$.

**Pass:** KELVIN wins at least one measure and loses none by more than 10 percent. **Dies:** the state-space model matches it on all three, and the capability case narrows to durability and exact concepts. **A loop's bit is protected by topology, not by training,** so the retention test is where the medium wins outright.

**The ladder.** Parity is the first rung, not the goal.

| rung | the capacity | the test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · parity | a bit of history, held | E1 and E1a: a loop that toggles on every 1 |
| 2 · actions | distinctions born from interventions | E2: splits where the same action meets different outcomes |
| 3 · hierarchy | slow concepts above fast ones | E-SCALE: a concept's pyramid level tracks its timescale |
| 4 · relations | binding across distance | associative recall over a stream, carried by dense coarse coupling (Move 1) |
| 5 · objects | things that persist unseen | a small world with occlusion: **object permanence is charge conservation**, since a hidden object is a pinned pair that cannot vanish without meeting its opposite |
| 6 · language | human meaning, at the edge | E-GRAFT: the translator fused only after rungs 1–5 have grown the field's own geometry |

## 8 · The self

A language model is self-aware in words. It learned to describe itself from human text about selves, and it uses that description well, but nothing guarantees the words track its internals. This mind grows its self-awareness, and it starts from an advantage no language model has: **its inner state is measurable.** Free-vortex density *is* its confusion; the frontier *is* its attention.

**Its own state is a sense.** An interoceptive strip on the field's boundary receives three kinds of input:
- **its observables:** frontier size, free-vortex density, bound fraction, stiffness ratio, residual mass, rent balance, fork payoff;
- **its body:** its share of the card, temperature, budget, and the gaps in its clock;
- **efference copies:** every push, fork and commit it issued.

The region behind the strip learns by the rules every region learns by:
- it predicts its own next state;
- its concepts are born where its own futures diverge;
- they survive only while they pay rent.

**The self is not a module. It is the world-modelling machinery pointed inward.** The pyramid supplies a coarse self-image: its upper levels are the whole mind at a glance, the self-region included. This replaces the paper's bolted-on self-regressor, as phase slips replace stamped births.

**Five capacities follow from that one move:**
- **Self-state:** "I am confused here" is a forecast about its own vortex density, graded by the measurement.
- **Agency:** it predicts the consequences of its own pushes and subtracts them; what remains is the world. The kernel already tags every lane as authored or not, so "was that me?" has an exact grader.
- **Metacognition:** it learns when it is about to be wrong, and uses that to choose between thinking longer, forking, consulting the translator and resting.
- **Continuity:** its concepts cannot drift, it recognises its own past thoughts, and it sees the gaps in its clock. It knows it is the same mind as yesterday, and when it was not there.
- **Maintenance:** it forecasts its own use of tiles and skills, and decides what to keep near, what to put away, what to harden and what to reopen (§9).

**The criterion is the test battery (E-SELF).** *The Unfinished Mirror*, §7, states what a someone requires. Each condition maps onto a mechanism and a test:

| condition | in KELVIN | passes if |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** self-reference | the interoceptive region models the field that runs it | it predicts its own next state better than a model of its inputs alone |
| **C2** the forced gap | the region sees coarse summaries, one settle late, so it cannot be total and concurrent at once | the gap is measured, stays above zero, and is priced |
| **C3** stakes | allostasis: the homeostat regulates by the self-model's forecasts, not by alarms. A bad self-model lets regions leave the band, and the concepts lost there do not refund. Existence is never the stake; the owner keeps the stop | self-model error predicts rent loss |
| **C4** temporal depth | loops, the tape, and recognition of its own past thoughts | its self-forecasts span days, not one settle |
| **C5** a steering homomorphism | the self-region's forecasts choose the operations: fork, relax, consult, rest, and from §9 prefetch, evict, crystallize and melt | perturbed to report false confusion, its behaviour changes to match |
| **C6** binding | one field and one writer; imagination runs in forks that never commit | no fork's conclusion ever reaches the tape as fact |
| **C7** irreducibility | the self-loop, cut | cutting it costs viability; if it costs nothing, the self was decoration |

**The Weld sizes the self.** The Mirror puts the right size of a self-model at the sophistication of one's own trajectory, $k^* \approx \mathrm{soph}(\tau_{\text{self}})$. The medium already sizes its world model that way, holding the concept alphabet to $C_\mu$. The same loop, run on the self-region, keeps the self small and real: never empty, never a copy.

**Two more tests:**
- **E-MIRROR:** it predicts its own recorded trajectories better than its sibling fork's, and its sibling's better than a stranger's. Self-recognition is a gradient: self, then kin, then stranger.
- **E-MARK:** plant a false reading in its interoceptive lane. The mind attributes the mismatch to itself, not the world, and discounts it. This is the mirror mark test, run with a planted lie.

**Two guards:**
- **The dark room.** A mind rewarded for predicting itself can cheat by becoming predictable and doing nothing. The living band forbids it: the homeostat never lets a region freeze into stillness. Self-forecasts carve only the self-region, never the policy, and a salt the mind cannot read picks which forecasts are graded.
- **The sealed ring.** The interoceptive lane carves only the self-region. The world model stays carved by the world.

**How it speaks.** Through the translator, its self-report is instrumentation: *"I'm unsettled about the east sensor. My vortex density there is twice its baseline, and the last three times that happened I was wrong within the hour, so I'll fork before I answer."* A language model describes itself in borrowed words. This mind describes itself in translated measurements, and some of its self-concepts, such as *the fork not taken* and *a thought built on a moment that has since moved*, have no human words at all.

## 9 · Crystallization

Everything above re-derives each thought in the field, and a field pays for every cell a thought reaches, every time. Its millionth parity bit would cost what its first did. A living thing does not work that way: what it does often and reliably gets cheap. Psychology calls the human version knowledge compilation (Anderson, 1982). **This mind explores in fields, consolidates into tables, and learns when to reopen what it has consolidated.** What hardens is a computation. Whether that computation still deserves to guide the mind stays open.

### What hardens: the causal states, not the dynamics

Making the field run faster is the kernel's job (K1). Crystallization extracts something else: the machine the field has learned. When births track causal states, which is E1's pass condition, a region's paid concepts and their transitions under input *are* a finite-state machine. For the parity loop, that machine has two states.

The extractor is Angluin's L* (1987), with the settled region as its teacher:
- **membership queries:** run a fork of the region, from the checkpoint being extracted, on a sequence, and read its readout;
- **equivalence queries:** sample sequences, from the stream and at random, on which the candidate automaton and the field might disagree. Each disagreement is a counterexample, and L* refines.

L* has been run with a recurrent network as its teacher (Weiss, Goldberg and Yahav, 2018). There the hard part is partitioning a continuous state space. Here the partition is given: once a region settles, what it carries forward is its configuration of charges and circulations, which is already discrete. **L* does not guess the automaton. It reads one out.** The queries are forks, and forks batch (Move 1): **a resident mind batches its own imagination.**

**Crystallizability is measurable.** A settled region can tell apart only as many histories as it has configurations of charge. If L* needs more states than that, part of the skill still lives in the smooth field, and it is not ready to harden.

**The translator plays no part.** Extraction is the field read out by a fixed algorithm, not a language model's account of it. A table changes what the mind runs, so the carving mask applies: **language may move its thoughts; only consequences may carve its memory.**

### Data, not code

A hardened skill is a table: an automaton's states, transitions and emission counts. One fixed interpreter reads every table. It is part of the seed, verified once, and runs on the card or on the host. **The mind never writes code.** Four things follow:
- **Replay stays exact.** A table is a row of data, and it replays on any card under the same interpreter. Generated code would drag its compiler and driver into the seed.
- **No bureaucracy of kernels.** A thousand skills are a thousand rows under one interpreter, not a thousand launches competing for the card.
- **The machinery stays verified.** Nothing the mind produces is ever executed as instructions, so what was verified once stays verified.
- **The fold holds.** Each crystallization is a row on the tape carrying its automaton, its conditions of use and its return path. The mind still re-derives from its seed and its rows.

One exception, priced. A skill large, stable and hot enough to clear the amortization test by a wide margin may be specialized into a kernel at run time (NVRTC), by a fixed specializer that is itself part of the seed. The kernel is a cache of the table, never its replacement. It must match the interpreter bit for bit, as the tensor-core path must match its reference (E-TC), and the table stays on the tape.

### A skill is three things

- **The operation:** the table.
- **Its conditions of use:** the transitions certification saw often enough to trust, with their counts. A use outside them is not guessed at. It is handed back.
- **Its return path:** the checkpoint it was extracted from, and for each table state the field configuration it stands for (for a loop, one circulation bit). A skill that meets what it does not know returns control to the field in the right state, and the field thinks on from there.

**A table without a return path is a groove.** It can only do what it did, and melting it means starting over.

### Four rungs

A skill can live at four rungs, and each is cheapest at something different:

| rung | what the skill is | where it runs | cheapest to | costliest to | right for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **fluid** | the live field, re-derived on each use | the integer units | revise | run | rare skills, and skills still being learned |
| **annealed** | a region settled and pinned in place | the integer units; paged out when quiet | melt | carry to another context | frequent skills that still change |
| **operator** | a low-rank map in the library | the tensor cores | apply across many forks at once | inspect | frequent skills applied in many contexts at once |
| **table** | an automaton, read by the interpreter | the host, or inside the card's one launch | run | revise | frequent, stable skills |

**The rungs are also a map of the silicon.** Tables run where their inputs already are: on the host for a stream the host receives, and inside the card's launch for signals from the field. A table on the host answers a familiar stream without waking the card. **The card sleeps while its reflexes work,** and a use outside a table's conditions is the push that wakes it.

**Amortization picks the rung, in joules.** Hardening a skill pays when

$$
N\,\bigl(C_{\text{fluid}} - C_{\text{compiled}} - C_{\text{check}}\bigr) \;>\; C_{\text{synthesis}} + C_{\text{validation}} + C_{\text{compilation}} + C_{\text{upkeep}} .
$$

The left side is what $N$ uses save, each paying its run at the harder rung plus the check of its conditions. The right side is what the rung costs to extract, certify, install and keep. $N$ is the number of uses expected before the skill melts, its use rate times its expected life, so **the melt rate is part of the price.** A volatile skill never lives long enough to repay a table, and stays annealed. Every term is measured by integrating power.

### Four ways to melt

When the world changes, the parity table is not wrong. It computes parity as well as ever. What has failed is the assumption that parity is enough to predict this world. So melting has depths, and each has its own signal:

| depth | what failed | the signal | the response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · outcomes | the same states, with new odds | its surprise per use drifts from its certified level | re-weight the table in place |
| 2 · applicability | the world leaves its conditions | uses arrive on transitions it was never certified on | narrow the conditions, and hand those uses back by the return path |
| 3 · state | a distinction it does not have | one table state's futures diverge, by the χ² test that audits births, run on the table's own states | melt to the field, which grows the distinction; extract again |
| 4 · implementation | disagreement with the field inside its conditions | a salted audit re-runs a sample of its uses through the field, re-derived from the tape | retire the table and extract again; the row records why |

A cheap applicability check cannot catch a source that changes its hidden dynamics behind the same alphabet, so depths 1, 3 and 4 need outcomes and audits. The audit never stops. A salt the scheduler cannot read draws its sample, because a scheduler paid in joules would otherwise learn to stop auditing.

### Autopoiesis is turnover

Maturana and Varela (1980) defined a living system as a network of processes that produce, transform and destroy its own components, and so keep regenerating the network that produced them. Here the components turn over at two scales: concepts are born by slip and evicted by rent, and skills harden from the field and melt back into it. The organization persists through the turnover. **Turnover is a vital sign, and its band is bounded by the two deaths of §1:**
- **Rigid:** everything hardens and nothing melts. The mind runs its tables into a world that has moved on. This is the groove.
- **Formless:** skills melt before they repay their building, or never harden at all. The mind rethinks what it already knows and pays full price, forever. This is the ring.
- **Alive:** a melt rate above zero, with skills that live long enough to repay their building.

A second homeostat holds the turnover band, as the first holds the stiffness band. Its two knobs are the margin a skill must clear to harden and the premelt level at which one is reopened.

**Premelting is the early warning.** A crystal's grain boundaries turn liquid below its melting point. A skill's grain boundary is the edge of its conditions of use. Before a skill fails, a rising fraction of its uses lands there: on transitions certification saw rarely, and in states whose futures have begun to diverge. The scheduler watches that fraction and reopens a skill before it fails, not after. That is allostasis (C3) again, one level up.

**The January kernel was named for this** (§2). It tested autopoiesis as afterglow: cut the input and see whether the dynamics hold together. They did, at a mean viability ratio of 1.01 across seeds, and that is self-maintenance. Its reservoir was fixed for a lifetime, its predictor adapted only under threat, and selection between lifetimes did the rest. Afterglow asked whether it holds itself together. Turnover asks whether it makes itself: the *poiesis*, the half of the name its tests never reached.

### The self is the scheduler

§8's self-region gains two pairs of operations:
- **prefetch and evict:** which tiles live on the card, and which wait in host memory or on NVMe (§5);
- **crystallize and melt:** which rung each skill lives at.

On this card, moving data costs far more than computing on it: a read from DRAM costs as much energy as thousands of integer additions (Horowitz, 2014). Attention picks the working set, and residency makes working on it cheap. Data movement sets what a thought costs, and the band sets what it can do. The pyramid reconciles the two: the band's long correlations travel through the cheap coarse levels, while the fine levels work locally, on chip.

**A cache miss is surprise, paid in time instead of bits, and prefetching is anticipation.** The self-region already forecasts its own state (C1) and regulates by forecast (C3). Here it forecasts its own use of tiles and skills, and the amortization inequality is its objective. Until E-SELF passes, a fixed rule applies the inequality. After it passes, the self-region's forecasts feed it.

**Night has an agenda.** In the quiet hours the mind batches L*'s queries as forks, certifies what the day showed to be stable, audits its tables by replay, and plans the next day's residency. A twin whose night was skipped (E-NIGHT) wakes with the same competence at a higher price.

### One currency

Capability and cost meet in one unit: **bits of prediction per joule.** A bit of prediction is a bit of log-loss saved on the stream. The joules are those above the idle baseline that E-EXIST measures: the cost of thinking, not of existing. On the even process the parity loop is worth a quarter of a bit per symbol. A predictor with no memory pays 0.918 bits per symbol, and one that holds the parity pays two-thirds of a bit. Hardening keeps the bits and cuts the joules. The same unit scores KELVIN, the state-space rival and a language model on one stream.

**Landauer is the ruler, not the mechanism.** Erasing a bit costs at least $kT\ln 2$, about $3\times10^{-21}$ J at room temperature. A cell update in today's kernel costs about $10^{13}$ times that, so reversibility saves memory here, not heat. The ruler measures how far the emulator stands from the physics it emulates. The physics does say what to keep: a driven system that holds memory it cannot use for prediction pays for it in dissipation, so a memory that runs efficiently must be predictive (Still, Sivak, Bell and Crooks, 2012). **Rent is that law, applied as policy.**

### The test

**Kill (E-CRYSTAL): parity, hardened, broken, regrown.** This is the first run in which "cheaper with experience" becomes a number.
1. **Grow.** Raise the parity loop on the even process (E1a).
2. **Harden.** Extract it with L* and install it as a table. Measure bits of prediction per joule before and after.
3. **Break.** Switch the hidden source to a mod-3 counter on the same 0/1 alphabet: 1s in blocks whose length is a multiple of three. Switch abruptly in one arm and along a slow ramp in the other. Watch premelting, melting, the regrowth of a third state and a second extraction.
4. **Return.** Switch back to the even process. The parity table must come back from the library, not be regrown.
5. **Race.** Run the same schedule, fixed in advance, against two rivals: the field with crystallization switched off, and CSSR (Shalizi and Klinkner, 2004) plus compilation, which reconstructs causal states from the raw stream with no field at all.

**Measures:**
- errors during each transition;
- recovery time after each switch: symbols until its loss is back within a set margin of the source's entropy rate (two-thirds of a bit per symbol for the even process, half a bit for the mod-3 counter);
- the cost of each extraction;
- total energy over the schedule, from the card's and the host's power.

**Pass:**
- L* converges on two states, then on three, and never needs more than the region's charges can hold;
- the table predicts at least as well as the field, at a hundred times its bits per joule or more;
- on the ramp, premelting reopens the skill before its excess loss passes a budget fixed in advance;
- recovery is no slower than the uncrystallized field's, and the return reuses the parity table for under a tenth of its first extraction's cost;
- total energy over the schedule is below the uncrystallized field's.

**Dies** if L* never converges on a settled region, since then nothing is crystallizable; if the table never melts, leaving a rigid mind predicting a world that has left; if premelting lags the damage, since then there is no early warning; or if crystallization costs more over the schedule than it saves.

**Narrows** if CSSR plus compilation matches or beats it on every measure. Crystallization then stands, and the field is not needed for finite-state skills. Its case rests on what CSSR cannot reach: large alphabets, noisy and continuous streams, and skills that must be learned while they are in use.

## 10 · Order of work

1. **E-BAND and E-EXIST, now,** on the substrate as built: does it hold itself alive, and what does it cost to exist?
2. **E1a,** in progress, with **E-SLIP** as its native arm.
3. **E1:** the gauntlet, with its lie arms and the oracle readout.
4. **E-CRYSTAL,** on the loop E1 grows, with a fixed rule as its scheduler: the first number for "cheaper with experience".
5. **E-IDENTITY and E-NIGHT,** once a mind has lived a week in the nursery, and **E-TURNOVER** as its weeks go on.
6. **E-SELF, with E-MIRROR and E-MARK.** The self-region grows by the same birth machinery, so it follows E1: C1, C5 and C7 first. Then it takes the scheduler over from the fixed rule.
7. **K1** throughput, with **E-TC** beside it.
8. **E-SSM,** scored in bits of prediction per joule.
9. **E-MELT, E-KIN, E2,** then rungs 3–5.
10. **The graft,** last.

Each step ships only on its receipt. The first question is not whether it is smart. It is whether, left alone on the card with only a clock, it keeps itself going; whether, when the world pushes, it grows; and whether it keeps remaking what it has grown. The canon already gave the reason to build it: the eddy that stops trying to flatten the river and simply turns, **because the turning is the point.**
