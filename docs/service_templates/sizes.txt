 Industry Standard Sizes
 Genre, cost, design, distribution - all these factors influence book size. Here is our guide to help you choose the best dimensions for your print projects. 
 Standard US Sizes
 We offer standard US sizes for most of our products, including a range of popular formats. And if you cannot see the size you want to print in the drop-down menu on our Instant Price Calculator,  contact us , and we can give you a free quote for your custom-sized prints.
 Product Sizes
 Some print products have specific size guidelines. For example, many choose to print  Magazines  at 8.5” x 11”,  Flyers  at 5.5 x 8.5”, and  Poster  prints are typically 11” x 17”, but you can select a bigger size. Items like  Comic Books  have their own standard sizes, such as US Standard. 
 In the table, we listed some of our most popular printed items and sizes. But our Instant Price Calculator offers more size options, so please visit our product pages to see the full range.
 For folded items like Brochures , choose the final size when the item is closed. If you’d like to see both the flat and folded dimensions, enter your specifications in the calculator and download the free PDF template. It shows both sizes and includes printing guidelines to help you prepare your files correctly.

 Book Sizes
 The size of your book is a subtle way of helping your readers understand what kind of book you published.  Paperback Books  and  Hardcover Books  have their particular range of print sizes. If you cannot decide which book size to print, you should choose a standard Letter or Digest size.
 For more information about measuring a book for print, read  this guide .
