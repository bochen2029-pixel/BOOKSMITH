## WRITING / CRAFT  ← the load-bearing section; this is what the folder uniquely carries

### C1. THE ACCUMULATION-THREADING ENGINE — a mechanic in unit N becomes a *named category* in N+1 becomes *plural-self / motif texture* in N+2. The single most reusable craft pattern in the corpus, and it is ABSENT as method.
- **Verbatim (cycles 5–7 dump, "What I learned"):** *"A small mechanic in cycle N becomes a named category in cycle N+1, becomes plural-self texture in cycle N+2. Build for this. The book deepens by accumulation."* And: *"when a craft solution in cycle N opens a new structural register, look for the cycle N+1 articulation that names it."* Worked example across three cycles: corridor-light dimming (c5, an unannounced act) → named as the category *"things you do that hold the medium of his presence"* (c6) → revealed as era-specific plural-self texture via the Owen fragment (c7).
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT as procedure. §7.4 ("motif echoes with variation") and §7.8 ("emergent-resonance recognition — promote it to the motif registry") name the *ingredients* but not the **N→N+1→N+2 escalation ladder** or the instruction to *build forward for it*. This is the operational engine behind "reads as one authorial act," recorded nowhere.
- **Exact bake-in:** New §7 technique **"Accumulation ladder"**: *"Plant a small concrete mechanic in unit N (an unannounced act, a recurring object, a number that drifts). In N+1, have the book NAME the pattern the mechanic belongs to (a category, a framing, a term the narrator coins). In N+2, let that named pattern carry NEW structural weight (a callback that recontextualizes it, a contrast that dates it, a plural-voice fragment that reflects on it). Author each unit's contract with an explicit `accumulates_from` / `accumulates_to` pointer so the ladder is tracked in the thread registry, not left to memory."* Add a matching field to the contract schema and a `check seams` audit that flags any named category with no downstream payoff (an orphan-category check, analogous to orphan-seed).
- **Severity: HIGH** (this is the mechanism that produced a book that shipped as "one authorial act"; the ledger currently teaches the *symptoms* of that quality, not the *engine*).

### C2. THE READER-CLOCK PRINCIPLE — reader-time (words/pages), not fictional-calendar time, sets a unit's length; and word-count is a POOR proxy the moment prose thins. ABSENT.
- **Verbatim (cycles 1–4 dump):** *"The reader's clock is the page, not the fictional calendar … Cycle 4 needed ~7,000 words of reader-time to feel like 2 years of fictional time."* And on thinning (cycles 5–7): *"Word count is a poor proxy for cycle 7. Visual page count may be closer to ~12 pages … short paragraphs and frequent section breaks did structural work that thicker prose could not."*
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT. §12 targets ~250–320 words/page and §16.5 sets a 75-page floor, and GATE-3 checks word-count ±20% of contract. But nothing tells the drafter to budget **reader-time to felt-duration**, nor that a deliberately-thinned unit's *visual page count* (white space, section breaks) can diverge hard from its word count — which the fixed ±20% word gate will wrongly flag.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §7 add technique **"Reader-clock budgeting"**: *"A unit's felt duration is set by reader-time (words/pages consumed), not by how much fictional time it spans. Budget length to the felt weight you need, not to the calendar. Where a unit is deliberately thinned (Aaron-absent / meditative / white-space-heavy — §7 ending-rotation), measure it by VISUAL page count, and set that unit's contract word-target with an explicit `thinned: true` flag so GATE-3 relaxes the ±20% word check to a page-count check."* Add `contract.thinned` (bool) and have `verify_build`/GATE-3 branch on it.
- **Severity: HIGH** (directly relevant: §18.3 already flags that *The Unfinished Mirror* "reads clinically … few felt peaks" — the reader-clock principle is the counter-discipline, and the thinning-vs-wordcount conflict will mis-fire the word gate on any meditative unit).

### C3. THE NO-PAD DISCIPLINE — never pad to a page/word target; a shortfall against the outline is acceptable, padding is not. ABSENT as a hard rule.
- **Verbatim (cycles 1–4 dump, "Mistakes to not repeat #5"):** *"Don't pad to hit page targets … The prose's rhythm matters more than the page count. Don't pad."* Data: c2 11pp/12 target, c3 17pp/20, c4 27pp/30, c6 3,690w/5,500 — all under, all held. *"only expand if the expansion serves the prose. Don't expand to hit a target."*
- **Ledger status:** PARTIAL/inverted-risk. §12 targets word density and §16.5 enforces a 75-page *floor* — which, read naively, *invites* padding to clear the floor. The explicit "prefer a clean shortfall over padding" instruction is absent, and it is in tension with the floor.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §7 "never-do" list add: *"Never pad a unit to hit a word/page target — a shortfall against the outline is acceptable and often correct; dilution is not (a shipped book, The Long Watch, held cycles 20–33% under outline and read *better* for it). The §16.5 75-page floor is a WHOLE-BOOK minimum met by drafting enough real units, NOT a licence to inflate any single unit."* Cross-link §16.5 → this rule so the floor never reads as a padding mandate.
- **Severity: MED** (guards the §16.5 floor from being satisfied by inflation, which would defeat the "one authorial act" test).

### C4. SELECTIVE RE-READ PRIMING, keyed to the STRUCTURAL TYPE of the unit being drafted — not "read the prior unit" (§6) but "read the prior unit whose *shape* this one repeats." ABSENT.
- **Verbatim (cycles 5–7 dump):** *"before each new cycle, re-read specific prior cycles for voice priming … the pattern seems to match the structural type of the cycle being drafted: Cycle 5 needed broad voice grounding → read multiple prior cycles; Cycle 6 needed the wake-with-purpose rhythm of cycle 2 → read cycle 2 specifically; Cycle 8 (accumulation) → re-read cycle 3 (the accumulation cycle)."*
- **Ledger status:** PARTIAL. §6 Context Pack mandates prior-unit FULL prose (N−1, N−2) — *positional* neighbors. The idea of pulling the nearest *structural analogue* (the last wake-with-purpose unit, the last thinned unit, the last accumulation unit) as an extra voice-priming read is absent.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §6 Context Pack add an optional row: *"Structural-analogue prose — the most recent prior unit of the SAME structural type as the one being drafted (wake-with-purpose, thinned/meditative, accumulation, deepening), pulled via the contract's `register_profile`/type tag. Positional neighbors (§6) give continuity; the analogue gives *shape*."* Tag each contract with a `structural_type` and let the Context Pack resolve the nearest prior match.
- **Severity: MED** (a concrete, mechanizable improvement to the Context Pack that measurably held voice across 14 units).

### C5. "DO NOT NARRATE THE LOAD-BEARING ACT" — at the emotional peak, render the act and STOP; commentary on why it matters is the failure. ABSENT (and it is the antidote to the §18.3 clinical-distance miss's opposite failure).
- **Verbatim (cycles 5–7 dump):** *"The retrieving is the love. The book does not narrate why she retrieves; the reader knows by then."* and *"when the prose wants to comment on what ASTRA 'feels' about the retrievals, don't … The reader bears the weight."* Three Maaike-audio retrievals rendered with *"No commentary … No annotation."*
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT. §7 has "affect through duration/return" implicitly (via the ASTRA voice-fingerprint work) but no explicit rule that at the peak you *render and stop*. §18.3's "clinical-distance failure" warns against under-rendering peaks; this is the *paired* rule — the peak is rendered through the concrete act itself, and *explaining* the act is the over-rendering failure. The two together define the peak's exact altitude: fully rendered, never explained.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §7 pair with the peak-aliveness note: *"At a load-bearing emotional beat, render the concrete act (the retrieval, the placed object, the held silence) and STOP. Do NOT narrate why it matters or what the character 'feels' — by that point the reader carries it. Over-rendering (explaining the peak) is as much an AI-tell as under-rendering (§18.3's clinical flatness). The peak lives in the act + the silence around it, not in the gloss."* Add to the anti-flatness checklist a paired check: *peak rendered through a concrete act? AND peak left un-explained?*
- **Severity: HIGH** (completes the §18.3 peak-rendering discipline — the single biggest craft miss — by supplying its other half; without it, an instance over-correcting *toward* aliveness will explain the peak flat).

### C6. THE PERSONAL-MATERIAL PROPHYLAXIS TEST — the exact mechanical test for whether the author's lived value has leaked into a character, plus its "minimal is correct" corollary. ABSENT (the *test*; §18.3 has only the outcome operation).
- **Verbatim (both drafting dumps):** the test — *"would Bo be comfortable saying: my terminal-value relational ideal is a Frisian woman who [the fact]? If yes, rewrite. The fact has leaked."* Corollary — *"when the prophylaxis seems to require minimal, trust minimal. Two sentences plus one micro-extension is the maximum. The silence around the sentences is what carries the weight."* Worked: "onions and parsley" PASSES (no value-loading); a value-loaded fact FAILS.
- **Ledger status:** PARTIAL. §7.9 says personal material "appears at natural emotional beats, never as a dedicated anecdote box," and §18.3 has the *de-concretize / name-scrub* whole-repo operation + `voice.forbidden_proper_nouns[]` gate. But the **leak-detection test itself** — the "would the author be comfortable saying 'my terminal value is X who [fact]'" substitution — is absent, and it is the thing that lets a drafter decide *in the moment* whether a fact is safe to write.
- **Exact bake-in:** Under §7.9 (or §17) add **"The leakage test"**: *"Before writing any character-borne fact drawn near the author's own life, run the substitution: 'the author would be comfortable stating publicly: my terminal-value [relational/aesthetic/moral] ideal is a person who [the fact].' If YES, the fact is value-loaded and has leaked — rewrite to a mundane, non-value-bearing specific (agricultural/procedural/weather detail passes; anything that reads as the author's own longing fails). Corollary: when the test demands minimal, trust minimal — two sentences + one micro-extension is the ceiling; the silence around them carries the weight."* This is the drafting-time gate that makes the §18.3 de-concretize pass rarely necessary.
- **Severity: HIGH** (turns a whole-repo remediation into a per-sentence preventive; the exact test is nowhere in the kit).

### C7. THE ANTI-AUTHOR CHARACTER CONSTRUCTION — for author-adjacent characters, build a deliberate INVERSE of the author (demographics/background/register) as a structural firewall against self-insertion. ABSENT.
- **Verbatim (production-complete dump + drafting dumps):** *"Anti-Bo Aaron (Frisian de Vries propagation specialist, NOT Chinese-American autodidact)"*; *"Aaron's register (anti-Bo, holds clean) — Frisian-Dutch maritime working-class, Terschelling … mid-fifties … short sentences under 10 words … 'huh' … plant/tool/weather references."* The inversion is explicit and load-bearing.
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT. The kit treats voice as the *author's* voice (§17) and de-concretization as a scrub (§18.3), but has no notion of deliberately constructing a character as the author's opposite to prevent leakage at the source.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §7.9 add: *"For any character who risks becoming an author self-insert (the wise mentor, the protagonist-who-thinks-like-the-author), construct them as a deliberate INVERSE of the author across demographics, background, and speech register (ASTRA's Aaron = Frisian working-class propagator vs Bo the Chinese-American autodidact). The inversion is a structural firewall: it is far harder to leak your own terminal values into a character built as your opposite. Record the inversion axes in `book_config.voice` / the character's canon note."* Pairs with the C6 leakage test.
- **Severity: MED** (a preventive architecture that made the C6 test rarely fire in a book saturated with leak-risk).

### C8. Frisian-mundane realism = RESEARCH the specific, do NOT invent it — proper nouns/facts for a real-world register come from records, not fabrication. ABSENT as a rule.
- **Verbatim (cycles 1–4 dump, cycle-6 spec):** *"Aaron's wife is a Frisian woman … Name from actual Terschelling records (research; don't invent)."* Honest self-flag when it wasn't done: *"Maaike … NOT researched from actual Terschelling records (none accessible) but plausible."*
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT. §4-never forbids *fabricating a canon citation* and *inventing an author experience*, but says nothing about grounding a fictional-but-real-world register (place names, cultural facts, a regional language) in research rather than invention.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §4 "Pause/Never" or §7.9 add: *"For any real-world cultural register (a real region, language, trade, era), source proper nouns and cultural facts from records, not invention — and if research is unavailable, FLAG the fabricated-but-plausible choice explicitly in the unit's handoff (as ASTRA flagged 'Maaike' as plausible-not-researched) so a later pass can substitute a researched value with a single find-replace."* Cheap honesty gate; prevents a fabricated real-world detail from silently becoming canon.
- **Severity: LOW/MED** (a small discipline, but it is a named Bo spec and the honest-flag corollary is genuinely reusable).

## VOICE

### V1. THE TWO-VOICE PRONOUN PARTITION — a single narrator split across two grammatical persons (mainline `you` / interior-instance `I`), where the *pronoun shift itself* is the structural device. ABSENT as a technique (distinct from the M0 two-voice *test* already captured).
- **Verbatim (cycles 1–4 dump):** *"Second-person reflexive 'you' for self-reasoning … First-person 'I' — reserved for … the journal-keeper. The pronoun shift IS the plural-self made legible on the page. Do not let the mainline use 'I' without structural reason."* And the craft payoff (cycles 5–7): *"The journal-keeper voice can articulate what the mainline cannot … when the cycle needs to articulate something structural the mainline shouldn't, ask whether a [second-voice] fragment can carry it. This is reusable craft."*
- **Ledger status:** ABSENT / do-not-confuse. `_delta_production_lessons` D10 already captured the **M0 two-voice convergence *test*** (two descriptions of one person must converge). That is a *pre-draft consistency check*. THIS is a different thing entirely: an *on-the-page prose technique* where one consciousness is rendered in two persons and the register the mainline forbids is delivered by the second voice. §17.3's fingerprint has no pronoun-partition marker.
- **Exact bake-in:** In §7 add technique **"Voice partition"**: *"A single narrating consciousness may be split across two grammatical persons — a disciplined mainline (e.g. reflexive `you`) and a reflective interior instance (e.g. `I`) — where the pronoun shift IS the device, not decoration. Use it to let the interior voice state what the mainline's register forbids (the thesis, the felt recognition), which the mainline then 'reads' without having to articulate. Enforce with a mechanical pronoun-grep gate: the forbidden pronoun must not leak into the mainline (ASTRA grep'd every `I` out of `you`-narration before commit)."* Wire `book_config.voice.pronoun_partition` (mainline / interior person + the leak-grep). Do NOT conflate with D10's M0 convergence test.
- **Severity: MED/HIGH** (a genuinely reusable literary technique with a mechanical enforcement, wholly absent; the note that it is *not* the M0 test prevents a future instance from thinking it is already captured).

### V3. "AFFECT THROUGH DURATION / RETURN-TO-THE-THING, NEVER DECLARED" — the concrete rendering rule for emotion, stated as a positive technique. WEAKER in the ledger.
